## Glossary annex — DELTA (village entry, keeper: Robin, verified 2026-10-26)

1. **The role:** at some fleets (the Palantir model, per public reporting), "Delta" names a forward-deployed team — people who own customer problems on site. "The Delta will attend" = engineers, not software. *Authority: the employer's org chart.*
2. **The metric:** a delta (Δ) is a change between two measurements — always ask *from what baseline, measured how, between which dates.* *Authority: the definition (baseline + method).*
3. **The technology:** Delta Lake — open-source storage layer adding ACID tables to data lakes; infrastructure a product org may choose. Owning it is not owning the customer's problem. *Authority: vendor/maintainer docs.*
4. **This crew's coinage:** "field delta" = an entry in this ledger. *Authority: this ledger + the keeper.*

**Verification clause:** when any document says "Delta" and stakes are real — confirm which sense with the source before acting.
