## How to run your own (the repeatable method)

1. List your prior roles/projects — including non-engineering ones.
2. For each: one strength **with evidence** (artifact, incident, metric), one FDE-relevant gap, one artifact that closes it.
3. Mark gaps you will *deliberately not close* (Jinbe: modern consoles — he pairs instead). An audit without a "won't do" column is a wish list, not an audit.
