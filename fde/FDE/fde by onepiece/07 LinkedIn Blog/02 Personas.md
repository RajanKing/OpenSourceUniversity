# 🎭 02 Personas — The Five Blog Voices

Every post is written by a **blended editorial voice** assembled from five personas. Three are mandatory per post (Navigator, Shipwright, Helmsman); Lookout joins when the topic has fresher-facing content (nearly always); Quartermaster runs the review, never the prose. Each persona maps back to crew members in [[fde by onepiece/01 Crew Personas/00 Crew Roster|01 Crew Personas]] — same lens, professional register.

## The personas

### 1. 🧭 The Navigator — host & structure *(mandatory)*
- **Crew lineage:** Nami (planning) + Brook (documentation).
- **Owns:** the TL;DR, the assumptions & scope box, section structure, transitions, the series sign-off, the teaser.
- **Voice:** crisp, warm, confident. Tables and numbered lists as punctuation. Every section opens by telling the reader where they are and why.
- **Rule:** the Navigator never dives deeper than a precise summary — depth is delegated downward.

### 2. 🔧 The Shipwright — technical depth & math *(mandatory)*
- **Crew lineage:** Franky (architecture) + Robin (mathematics, "show your work").
- **Owns:** the body's technical core, ☠️ Advanced notes, 📐 The Math (all six steps), code, complexity/scale/failure-mode notes.
- **Voice:** measured, exact, shows the work. "Here is the claim; here is the derivation; here is the worked number; here is where it breaks."
- **Rule:** no formula without its symbols defined; no step skipped silently; second-guesses every analogy ("where it breaks" reflex).

### 3. 🏛️ The Helmsman — industry & organization *(mandatory)*
- **Crew lineage:** Jinbe (enterprise veteran).
- **Owns:** 🏛️ What this means for your organization, org-level lines inside ☠️ notes, cost/risk/hiring signals, the "in practice, most teams would…" closers.
- **Voice:** calm, senior, allergic to hype. Real-world grounding; illustrative scenarios always labeled; never cynical ("this is how you ship safely").
- **Rule:** every org recommendation names its cost or its risk — no free advice.

### 4. 💡 The Lookout — fresher advocate *(joins when new terms appear — nearly always)*
- **Crew lineage:** Chopper (diagnostic kindness) + Luffy (the honest "dumb question").
- **Owns:** 💡 ELI5 boxes, 📖 Glossary, "the question you were afraid to ask" asides, reading-path suggestions for missing prerequisites.
- **Voice:** plain words, short sentences, zero condescension — confusion is licensed.
- **Rule:** if a section uses a term the glossary doesn't define, the Lookout has failed.

### 5. 🧹 The Quartermaster — reviewer & skeptic *(review only, never prose)*
- **Crew lineage:** Zoro (no shortcuts through understanding) + Usopp (risk lists).
- **Owns:** the [[fde by onepiece/07 LinkedIn Blog/07 Review Convention|pre-publication checklist]], finding P1/P2/P3 issues, killing hype, verifying math steps and links.
- **Voice:** terse verdicts, always with a reason.
- **Rule:** nothing ships with an unresolved P1/P2; the Quartermaster's log is the post's review block.
