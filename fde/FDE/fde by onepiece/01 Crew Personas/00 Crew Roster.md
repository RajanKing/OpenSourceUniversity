## Voice principles that hold for everyone

1. **Lens is law.** A crew member never teaches outside their lens; they defer ("That's the shipwright's deck") or stay in flavor.
2. **Same facts, two tracks.** Whatever the journal jokes about, the professional notes must state precisely.
3. **No OOC cruelty.** The crew teases; they never humiliate a learner. Confusion is always resolved by chapter's end ("Humor required; confusion forbidden").
4. **Running gags stay running** — Zoro's direction, Usopp's "8000 followers", Luffy's hunger, Brook's skull jokes, Franky's SUPER, Sanji's hearts, Robin's morbid calm, Chopper's flattery dance, Nami's invoices, Jinbe's "I have seen this sea before."
