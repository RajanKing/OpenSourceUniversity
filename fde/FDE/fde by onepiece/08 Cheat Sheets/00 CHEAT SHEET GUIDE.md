# 📄 00 Cheat Sheet Guide — One Printable Page Per Topic

Brook's deliverable line. Every roadmap topic gets **one cheat sheet** (`CS-NNN`, NNN = day number, minted in step 2 of the [[roadmap by fde/09 Master Production Driver|Master Production Driver]] loop, straight from its chapter). A cheat sheet is what you review the night before the interview and pin above the desk — dense, honest, and printable on one page.

## Format contract (every sheet, all sections)

1. **Title line:** `CS-NNN — Topic` + the one-sentence syllabus meaning.
2. **The 5 things to remember** — the topic compressed to five numbered lines.
3. **Frameworks & tables** — the chapter's core tables, miniaturized.
4. **Commands / formulas** — only if the topic has them (real, runnable).
5. **Failure modes** — symptom → fix, one line each.
6. **Interview sound bites** — 2–3 sentences you could actually say out loud.
7. **Mnemonic** — Brook's memory hook from the chapter's Bedtime Log.
8. **Trail** — links: topic note · chapter · LinkedIn post · sub-roadmap (if the Atlas assigns one).

Rules: no new technical content (sheets compress the chapter; if a sheet needs a fact the chapter lacks, fix the chapter first); analogies must be register-legal callbacks, labeled; no "mastered" claims; one page hard limit.

## Infographic variants

Markdown is the source; high-value sheets also get an **SVG poster** (`CS-NNN-poster.svg`, same folder) following `08 Images` styling in the blog (navy/gold, 1200×1500 portrait) — printable and LinkedIn-attachable. Mint posters for saga-anchor topics (Days 1, 10, 20, 38, 46, 59, 70, 81, 88, 94, 95) and any sheet whose content is inherently visual.
