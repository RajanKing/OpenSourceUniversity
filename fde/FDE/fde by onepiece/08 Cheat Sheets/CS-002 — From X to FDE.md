# CS-002 — From X to FDE: The Transition Playbook

**Meaning:** the transition builds on existing strengths and closes gaps in customer discovery, AI reliability, and production ownership — with evidence, not certificates.

## The 5 things to remember

1. **Audit before you shop** — five columns: prior role · carries over (*with evidence*) · gap · **closing artifact** · deliberately won't learn. No artifact, no row.
2. **Ambiguity dies to one question** — "What happens if it's never fixed — for whom, by when?" (RAR: Referent, Impact, Horizon).
3. **The portfolio's four species** — working system · decision log · measured result · honest failure analysis. Certificates verify attendance; artifacts verify delivery.
4. **Judge the wake, not the figurehead** — trust causal traces on a schedule; visible-but-idle elements prove nothing.
5. **Read the fight, not the gym list** — a JD's first line is the job; the requirement column is the recruiter's wish. Three postings' intersection is the real spec.

## Frameworks

**RAR triad:** Referent (*which one, walk me to it*) · Impact (*what breaks, for whom*) · Horizon (*by when*). The impact question is the keystone — it forces a story, not an adjective.

**Artifact species bar:** written for a stranger ("readable in the lamplight of someone who owes you nothing") · evidence-linked · failure section included · customer-stamped where possible.

**5-step JD triage:** pull the fight (summary + first bullets, verbatim) → split the column (mandatory = restated in fight or ×2) → rank by evidence (carry/thin/gap) → mint closing artifacts only for mandatory gaps → 90-day plan as artifacts, not courses.

**Systems table:** inputs · actors · tool · environment · feedback loops — evaluation attaches a trace + schedule + threshold to each loop.

## Commands

`python quest_log.py add --title "…" --requester "…" --impact "what breaks, for whom, if never done"` — the impact note is ambiguity reduction made executable (optional, echoed, analyzable; additive change — format never bumped without a migration).

## Failure modes

- Eleven certificates, zero repos → audit by consumption → every claimed capability must cite an artifact.
- Restatement precise but requester goes quiet → conflict, strategic vagueness, or shame — surface it; never average it into mush.
- Usage up, outcomes flat → wrong trace or Goodhart rot → pair quantity with a quality check.

## Interview sound bites

- "My transition plan is an audit with evidence per row and a won't-learn list — here are the three artifacts that closed my top gaps."
- "The decision log is the artifact you can't fake quickly — rejected alternatives, dated, written the day I chose."

## Mnemonic

🎵 *Nobody's born on the Grand Line — we all sailed in from outside. Count what you're carrying, sharpen the vague, build pages for strangers, read wakes, and study the port you sail to.* 🎵
