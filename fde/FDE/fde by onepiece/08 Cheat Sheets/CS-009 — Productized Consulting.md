# CS-009 — Productized Consulting: Machine, Crew, or Catalog

**Meaning:** productized consulting packages **repeatable delivery knowledge into bounded offerings without hiding customer-specific uncertainty.**

## The 5 things to remember

1. **Define a repeatable outcome** — a verifiable end-state ("operator and deputy run, back up, diagnose, recover unaided — proven by witnessed rehearsal"), with a **proof-of-repetition line**: every claim cites a shipped artifact. Packaging from memory = a packaged fantasy.
2. **Two columns make it honest** — the **kit** (what you bring: software, runbooks, templates, seed, training) and the **homework** (what the customer owes: sponsor, machine, operator+deputy, records-keeper introduced, consents). Fixed price is only honest when the inputs are fixed too; slippage pauses the clock *visibly*.
3. **Exclusions + change control, signed before work** — "forever" is the most expensive word in the sea. New scope = written, priced, initialed — never absorbed. And hand the customer the pen for *your* page: signature order is honesty order.
4. **Reuse tested components** — same versioned core, declared configuration; hull-thinking. The kit ships as a crate; the demo seed **refuses non-empty stores** (an engagement repeats; it never double-writes).
5. **Feed the pattern into the product** — one harbor is a story, two a sign, three a roadmap. File the second harbor as a delta with checkable evidence; arm the tripwire.

## Frameworks

**The page:** outcome sentence (state, not features) → proof-of-repetition → kit → homework → exclusions → change control → escalation. **The four boundary tools:** exclusions · change control · conditions ("ten days *given* these inputs") · the prototype clause.

**The third-harbor clause:** D-002's tripwire — pattern at two harbors files; a third promotes to the roadmap.

## Commands

`python quest_log.py demo --file data/foosha.json` — seeds 3 standard synthetic quests into a FRESH store (exit 0); refuses non-empty targets untouched (exit 1). An engagement repeats; it never double-writes.

## Failure modes

- The everything brochure (fixed price + "forever") → homework column + exclusions, signed.
- The packaged fantasy (page written from memory) → every claim cites an artifact.
- The absorbing habit ("sure, we can add that") → change control with numbers and signatures.
- The ossified kit (reuse without the feed) → delta ledger + change-controlled versions.
- **The silently swallowed test** (a guard who resigned) — count your tests against your run, every time.

## Interview sound bites

- "You're not buying my week; you're buying my method — and here's the proof it repeats."
- "One village is an engagement, two is a pattern, three is a roadmap."

## Mnemonic

🎵 *One page to sell you, and I'll tell you what it's not: not forever, not everything, not a promise I forgot.* 🎵
