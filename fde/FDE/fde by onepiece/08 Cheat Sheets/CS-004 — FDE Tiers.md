# CS-004 — FDE Tiers: Stripes Are Fleet-Local

**Meaning:** FDE labels and tiers are employer-specific descriptions of technical scope, customer ownership, and product influence — **not** a universal credential ladder.

## The 5 things to remember

1. **The spectrum** — implementation-heavy (judged on shipped systems) ↔ research-adjacent (judged on defensible knowledge). Locate with four probes: weekly output · definition of done · failure shape · consumers.
2. **Autonomy = decision rights** — what you may conclude *alone* vs. what you must *escalate* (watch levels). Ask any "senior" seat for its escalation matrix.
3. **Two rulers, one career** — seniority (cumulative general trust) × domain specialization (verified waters sailed). Hiring gates on both; one doesn't buy the other.
4. **Read the race** — a rubric is the panel's scoring function (what's verified, what's weighted, the fossil row = what recently burned them). Reconstruct it before you prep.
5. **Comp reports are fish-market boards** — dated, market-specific, methodology-bound. Five-question check (when/where/who/sample/split); ranges and trends, never point-value promises.

## Frameworks

**Autonomy ladder (typical):** T1 executes tasks, escalates scope · T2 owns a workstream's approach · T3 owns the engagement, sets approach · T4 owns the portfolio, sets strategy. Read bottom-up: each tier = the previous tier's evidence, compounded.

**Rubric reconstruction kit:** ① what does the panel verify? ② visible weights? ③ what recently burned them (the hyper-specific must-have)? ④ what does a great first 90 days mean *there*? ⑤ judgment artifacts or survival counts?

**Comp check:** date collected · geography · sample source (self-reports inflate, posted ranges anchor high) · base/equity/bonus split · trend & driver. Halving test: would the plan survive the number halving?

## Commands

`python quest_log.py stats` — the Sunday read, automated (pure `summarize()`: total/open/done, distinct requesters, latest activity). Evidence you must hand-count eventually stops being counted.

## Failure modes

- Week-two surprise ("this isn't what FDEs do") → title anchoring → run the four probes + rubric reconstruction *pre-offer*.
- "I'm senior anywhere" → one-ladder thinking → two-axis map, evidence per cell, state the domain transfer tax.
- Strong answers, failed loops → wrong-race prep → answer the fossil row, not the JD.
- Life decisions on one unsplit number → gazette navigation → five-question check + your own dated comparables.

## Interview sound bites

- "Tier names are portable; tier meanings are not — before chasing a stripe, learn which fleet draws it and with what ruler."
- "Price your evidence, not your title. Titles inflate; evidence compounds."

## Mnemonic

🎵 *Stripes are the fleet's, but the wake is the water's — and water keeps records wherever you go.* 🎵
