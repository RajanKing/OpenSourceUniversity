# CS-003 — Roles & Responsibilities: Who Answers

**Meaning:** responsibilities span technical depth and field ownership; the balance varies by employer — the span never does. Every delivery activity has exactly one accountable owner and an explicit handoff condition.

## The 5 things to remember

1. **Operators give truth; sponsors give survival** — classify by what they can take away (cooperation quietly vs. the whole engagement politely). Ask operators "walk me through yesterday"; ask sponsors "what would make you pull the plug?"
2. **Audit below the waterline** — every store, copy, pipe, consumer, owner — plus what breaks *silently*. The official diagram is marketing; the shadow spreadsheet is the system.
3. **A slice passes four gates** — vertical · narrow · valuable · **owned**. One league under sail beats a fleet of half-ships.
4. **Handover is a ceremony, not a mat** — deploy → observe → document → transfer; the passing grade is the **silence test**: a named owner operates an unaided restart while you say nothing.
5. **Delegate the lighthouse, never the wreck** — tasks move freely; accountability moves only by re-signed handoff. An impressive prototype transfers nothing.

## Frameworks

**Matrix row anatomy:** Accountable (**one** name) · Does the work (tasks — delegable) · Consulted/Informed · **Explicit handoff condition** (dated or observable). Plus: deputy per owner; review cadence tied to an existing ritual; re-sign on any name/tool/constraint change.

**Five audit questions:** where does data live (incl. unofficial) · what moves via what · what consumes it · who owns each store/pipe · what breaks silently.

**Task vs. accountability:** "security ran the pen test" = a task done; "the release is secure at this customer" = an outcome someone names answers for. Teams don't answer pages; a name in a rotation does.

## Commands

`python quest_log.py close --id N --by "Name" --note "what was done"` — named closure, timestamped, double-close refused: accountability made executable (12/12 tests executed).

## Failure modes

- Matrix contradicts reality by week three → re-read at every instrument read; re-sign on changes.
- Crew name in every accountable cell → accountability hoarding; sponsor powers back to the sponsor; every crew row gets a dated exit.
- "Team Y has it" with no ticket/ack/unchanged name → lanyard handoff → point to all three or it's a shrug.
- Friendly handover email, six months later an incident → no silence test, no re-sign on name changes.

## Interview sound bites

- "One name per row, handoff condition per row — a matrix without exits is tenancy, not engagement."
- "I stay reachable *priced and limited*: a written on-call tail that lapses by its own condition."

## Mnemonic

🎵 *Hands you lend and hands you borrow, the wreck is on your log — sign your name where you can stand, and stand there like a rock.* 🎵
