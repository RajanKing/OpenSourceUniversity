## Topics and Evidence



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Enterprise Workflow|Enterprise Workflow]]

Enterprise workflows combine people, systems, approvals, exceptions, and audit requirements across organizational boundaries.

- Evidence: Every state transition, decision authority, and recovery path has a clear owner.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/ROI and AI Impact|ROI & AI Impact]]

ROI estimates whether realized benefits exceed full implementation and ongoing costs; AI impact also includes quality, risk, and adoption.

- Evidence: The business case states assumptions, uncertainty, measured inputs, and the threshold for a worthwhile deployment.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Stakeholder Management|Stakeholder Management]]

Stakeholder management aligns people with different authority, incentives, risks, and daily responsibilities.

- Evidence: The right people approve the right decisions and unresolved disagreement is visible before release.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Business Acumen|Business Acumen]]

Business acumen connects engineering effort to the customer's economics, incentives, constraints, and operating model.

- Evidence: Explain who benefits, who pays, what changes operationally, and what evidence would invalidate the business case.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Product Feedback Loop|Product Feedback Loop]]

A product feedback loop turns repeated field evidence into prioritized product changes and verifies whether those changes help.

- Evidence: A product decision is linked to customer evidence and the shipped change is checked against the original problem.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Time-to-Value|Time-to-Value]]

Time-to-value measures elapsed time until a customer experiences a defined useful outcome, not until a demo is shown.

- Evidence: The start, value event, cohort, exclusions, and sustained adoption check are explicit.
