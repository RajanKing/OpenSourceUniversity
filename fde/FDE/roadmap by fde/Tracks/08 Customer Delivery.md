## Topics and Evidence



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Requirements Gathering|Requirements Gathering]]

Requirements describe observable needs and constraints, including the exceptions that determine whether a system is usable.

- Evidence: Each critical requirement maps to a demonstration, measurement, test, or documented review.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Scoping and Sequencing|Scoping & Sequencing]]

Scoping limits the work; sequencing orders it to resolve the highest-impact uncertainty before expensive commitments.

- Evidence: The first milestone tests a real project-killing assumption and later work depends on explicit evidence.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Scope, Speed, Quality|Scope, Speed, Quality]]

Delivery tradeoffs require explicit choices about breadth, schedule, and assurance; critical safety constraints are not bargaining chips.

- Evidence: A written tradeoff names what is deferred, what remains mandatory, and who accepts the residual risk.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Discovery and Scoping|Discovery & Scoping]]

Discovery converts an ambiguous desire for AI into a bounded problem with users, evidence, constraints, and an explicit success test.

- Evidence: Stakeholders agree on the problem, decision owner, pilot boundary, and evidence needed to continue.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Technical Writing|Technical Writing]]

Technical writing makes designs, operation, and decisions understandable to the person who must use them later.

- Evidence: A new engineer can deploy, test, diagnose, and recover the supported workflow from the documentation.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Communication|Communication]]

FDE communication keeps technical and business participants aligned on decisions, evidence, blockers, and ownership.

- Evidence: Each audience can state the current outcome, key risk, next action, and responsible owner.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Executive Reporting|Executive Reporting]]

Executive reporting summarizes business outcomes, risk, and decisions at a level that supports action.

- Evidence: A sponsor can decide whether to continue, change scope, or stop based on the report.
