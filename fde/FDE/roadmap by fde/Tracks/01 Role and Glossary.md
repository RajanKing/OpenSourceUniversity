## Topics and Evidence



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Introduction|Introduction]]

An FDE turns a customer's operational problem into a working, adopted software system while feeding reusable lessons back into the product.

- Evidence: Explain the engagement from business pain to operating ownership, including a reason to stop the project.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Roles and Responsabilities|Roles & Responsabilities]]

Responsibilities span technical depth and field ownership; the exact balance depends on the employer and engagement.

- Evidence: Every delivery activity has an accountable owner and an explicit handoff condition.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/From X to FDE|From X to FDE]]

A transition into FDE work builds on existing engineering strengths and closes gaps in customer discovery, AI reliability, and production ownership.

- Evidence: A portfolio includes a working system, decision log, measured result, and honest failure analysis.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/FDE Tiers|FDE Tiers]]

FDE labels and tiers are employer-specific descriptions of technical scope, customer ownership, and product influence, not a universal credential ladder.

- Evidence: Explain which roles fit your evidence today and the concrete gaps for the next scope of ownership.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Delta|Delta]]

In this FDE glossary, Delta refers to terminology associated with a field-deployed engineering model; it is not automatically the Delta Lake storage format.

- Evidence: Resolve the meaning from context and avoid treating one company's terminology as an industry standard.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Last-Mile Integration|Last-Mile Integration]]

Last-mile integration makes a general product useful inside a customer's actual identities, data, approvals, and day-to-day tools.

- Evidence: A user completes the real task in the target workflow, including error and fallback paths.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Day 2 Operations|Day 2 Operations]]

Day 2 begins after launch and covers reliability, maintenance, incidents, access changes, cost, and continuous improvement.

- Evidence: Another engineer can detect, diagnose, recover, and communicate an incident using the runbook.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/UAT|UAT]]

User acceptance testing asks whether representative users can complete agreed business tasks safely, not merely whether automated tests pass.

- Evidence: Business owners sign off on documented workflows, limitations, unresolved defects, and release conditions.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Productized Consulting|Productized Consulting]]

Productized consulting packages repeatable delivery knowledge into bounded offerings without hiding customer-specific uncertainty.

- Evidence: An engagement can be repeated with consistent artifacts while exceptions remain explicit.



### [[roadmap by fde/Topics/Embedded Engineering|Embedded Engineering]]

Embedded engineers work closely with customer teams to learn the operational context and deliver changes within it.

- Evidence: The customer can operate the delivered workflow and the product team receives reusable requirements.
