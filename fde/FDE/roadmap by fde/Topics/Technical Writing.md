# Technical Writing

[[roadmap by fde/00 Start Here|Start Here]] / [[roadmap by fde/Tracks/08 Customer Delivery|08 Customer Delivery]] / [[roadmap by fde/01 Complete Topic Index|Complete Topic Index]]

## Meaning and FDE Relevance

Technical writing makes designs, operation, and decisions understandable to the person who must use them later.

## Concepts to Understand

- Identify reader and task.
- Define terms.
- Document prerequisites.
- Include runnable examples where appropriate.
- Explain failure and recovery.
- Keep architecture decisions and runbooks current.

## Applied Exercise

Write a setup guide and an outage runbook, then have someone unfamiliar with the project follow them.

Use synthetic data and sandbox services. Do not connect customer systems, spend provider credits, or change production data without explicit authorization.

## Common Failure Mode

Describing intended behavior without configuration, validation, or recovery steps.

## Evidence of Mastery

A new engineer can deploy, test, diagnose, and recover the supported workflow from the documentation.

- [ ] Explain the concepts and tradeoffs without reading the notes.
- [ ] Complete the exercise and retain a reproducible artifact.
- [ ] Test an error, permission, or recovery case where applicable.
- [ ] Record an observation, limitation, and next improvement.
