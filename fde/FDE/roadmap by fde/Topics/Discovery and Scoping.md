# Discovery & Scoping

[[roadmap by fde/00 Start Here|Start Here]] / [[roadmap by fde/Tracks/08 Customer Delivery|08 Customer Delivery]] / [[roadmap by fde/01 Complete Topic Index|Complete Topic Index]]

## Meaning and FDE Relevance

Discovery converts an ambiguous desire for AI into a bounded problem with users, evidence, constraints, and an explicit success test.

## Concepts to Understand

- Observe the current workflow.
- Identify painful decisions.
- Establish a non-AI baseline.
- Audit accessible data.
- Identify sponsor and operator needs.
- Define exclusions and stop criteria.

## Applied Exercise

Interview an operator and sponsor, then write a pilot brief with one workflow, one primary metric, assumptions, and a go/no-go review.

Use synthetic data and sandbox services. Do not connect customer systems, spend provider credits, or change production data without explicit authorization.

## Common Failure Mode

Starting with a preferred agent architecture rather than a validated customer problem.

## Evidence of Mastery

Stakeholders agree on the problem, decision owner, pilot boundary, and evidence needed to continue.

- [ ] Explain the concepts and tradeoffs without reading the notes.
- [ ] Complete the exercise and retain a reproducible artifact.
- [ ] Test an error, permission, or recovery case where applicable.
- [ ] Record an observation, limitation, and next improvement.
