# Requirements Gathering

[[roadmap by fde/00 Start Here|Start Here]] / [[roadmap by fde/Tracks/08 Customer Delivery|08 Customer Delivery]] / [[roadmap by fde/01 Complete Topic Index|Complete Topic Index]]

## Meaning and FDE Relevance

Requirements describe observable needs and constraints, including the exceptions that determine whether a system is usable.

## Concepts to Understand

- Separate business goals from solution ideas.
- Capture functional and nonfunctional needs.
- Document data and permission constraints.
- Write acceptance criteria.
- Prioritize exceptions.
- Resolve conflicting stakeholder needs.

## Applied Exercise

Convert interview notes into requirements with examples, failure cases, priority, owner, and verification method.

Use synthetic data and sandbox services. Do not connect customer systems, spend provider credits, or change production data without explicit authorization.

## Common Failure Mode

Recording vague statements such as accurate, secure, or fast without testable meaning.

## Evidence of Mastery

Each critical requirement maps to a demonstration, measurement, test, or documented review.

- [ ] Explain the concepts and tradeoffs without reading the notes.
- [ ] Complete the exercise and retain a reproducible artifact.
- [ ] Test an error, permission, or recovery case where applicable.
- [ ] Record an observation, limitation, and next improvement.
