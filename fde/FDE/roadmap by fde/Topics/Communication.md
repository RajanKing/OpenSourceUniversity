# Communication

[[roadmap by fde/00 Start Here|Start Here]] / [[roadmap by fde/Tracks/08 Customer Delivery|08 Customer Delivery]] / [[roadmap by fde/01 Complete Topic Index|Complete Topic Index]]

## Meaning and FDE Relevance

FDE communication keeps technical and business participants aligned on decisions, evidence, blockers, and ownership.

## Concepts to Understand

- Adapt detail to audience.
- Lead with the decision or outcome.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Communicate blockers early.
- Confirm understanding.
- Record actions, owners, and dates.

## Applied Exercise

Write an operator update, an engineering incident note, and an executive summary about the same failed integration.

Use synthetic data and sandbox services. Do not connect customer systems, spend provider credits, or change production data without explicit authorization.

## Common Failure Mode

Hiding uncertainty behind jargon or reporting activity without saying what decision is needed.

## Evidence of Mastery

Each audience can state the current outcome, key risk, next action, and responsible owner.

- [ ] Explain the concepts and tradeoffs without reading the notes.
- [ ] Complete the exercise and retain a reproducible artifact.
- [ ] Test an error, permission, or recovery case where applicable.
- [ ] Record an observation, limitation, and next improvement.
